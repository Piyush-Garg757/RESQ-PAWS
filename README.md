# RESQ-PAWS🐕🐾
Stray animal rescue efforts face significant delays due to inefficient helpline systems, lack of urgency-based case prioritization, inaccurate or incomplete incident reporting, and reliance on manual documentation. These challenges hinder timely intervention, leading to prolonged animal distress and missed rescue opportunities. Streamlining processes and integrating technology can greatly improve response times and overall rescue effectiveness.

ResQ PAWS automates rescue reporting with GPS and AI-based case severity, instantly alerting NGOs. Its 24/7 digital dashboard streamlines case management and cuts operational costs. Pet Adoption feature connects rescued animals with adopters through verified profiles and real-time updates.

KEY FEATURES
AI-Powered Automated Case Processing – Instantly processes reports by forwarding images and locations to NGOs, reducing response times from hours to minutes.
Smart Injury Classification & Prioritization – AI categorizes injuries as severe, moderate, or minor, ensuring critical cases receive immediate attention.
Precise Location Tracking – Enhances rescue speed, allowing rescuers to locate animals 10x faster than traditional methods.
24/7 Availability & Scalability – Operates continuously, enabling NGOs to handle more cases efficiently without extra costs.
Integrated Pet Adoption System – Showcases rescued animals for adoption with verified profiles, health updates, and seamless NGO coordination.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
AI Model for Image Analysis – Classifies injury severity from images.
GPS Tracking System – Captures and shares exact locations.
WEBSITE DESIGN- HTML , JAVASCRIPT, CSS, TAILWIND, BOOTSTRAP , MONGO DB, NODE.JS, AWS.
